Use this phone's microphone

Phones only let a page use the microphone over a secure connection, so this page needs to open its secure address first.

Your browser will then warn that the connection isn't private. That's expected: it is the console on the laptop. Tap Advanced (or Show details), then Proceed (or visit this website). This phone only has to do that once.

Secure connection ready. Tap Listen at the bottom to start. Keep this page open and the phone unlocked and plugged in; place it near the ragis' speaker.
